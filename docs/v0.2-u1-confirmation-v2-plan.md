## Why a successor is necessary

The first U1 confirmation attempt completed normally as a **qualification failure**, not as a
negative capability result. Its immutable report is:

  `/Volumes/T7 Developer/DungeonApprentice/confirmations/v0.2-u1-20260723/report.json`
- report SHA-256:
  `d2fa53308f7488cc08f5ee67b86a125ad91c6ba3790fcd9433c35aab1215b25c`
- verdict: `qualification_failed`
- checkpoint scoring performed: `false`

The fixed v1 blocks contained exact same-lesson reference collisions. Each lineage encountered the
same block-level violation: 8 of 200 Visible Unlock cases overlapped its frozen validation suite,
and 1 of 200 Local Unlock cases overlapped the Local Unlock engineering-qualification suite. The
v1 protocol correctly stopped before loading or scoring a policy. Its report therefore contains no
evidence for or against checkpoint capability and remains the authoritative record of that
attempt; v2 does not overwrite, reinterpret, or quietly retry it.

The canonical launcher requires that report at the path above and measures its SHA-256 before
opening a successor candidate. The evaluator authenticates the immutable content identity, so an
auditor may use a relocated byte-identical copy only if it has the same frozen SHA-256. A missing
or mismatched report is an integrity failure. Although v1 never scored a policy, its 600 opened
qualification layouts are now prior evidence. Their same-lesson exact-layout hashes are excluded
from v2 selection.

The finite procedural generators can repeat exact layouts even when numerical seed ranges are
disjoint. V2 addresses that discovered measurement-design flaw by freezing a deterministic,
policy-blind rejection rule over substantially larger candidate streams. It does not change a
checkpoint, pass threshold, evaluation behavior, or learning algorithm.

## Question

Do the three already selected U1 mastery checkpoints retain Navigate, Visible Unlock, and Local
Unlock on deterministic, reference-disjoint 200-case panels that played no role in training,
promotion, mastery selection, or replication reporting?

This is post-training confirmation. It never changes a policy, optimizer, curriculum, recurrent
state, reward, or generator. A positive result confirms replicated cumulative learning on the
Local Unlock distribution; it does not establish U2, unrestricted Unlock, Retrieve, or final-suite
generalization.

## Deterministic panel construction

Panel construction is a generator-and-oracle operation only. Before selection, the evaluator
authenticates the frozen checkpoint archives, sidecars, lineage metadata, and exact-layout history
files needed for the declared exclusions. It does not deserialize a model or load a policy tensor,
optimizer state, recurrent state, policy behavior, or policy score until all three panels have been
selected and qualified. Policy behavior and score are therefore unavailable to the selector and
can never influence acceptance. Checkpoint identities and the permitted exact-layout reference
artifacts are frozen in advance.

The selector may read only the frozen reference artifacts needed to build its predeclared hash
sets: same-lesson validation layouts, the U1 engineering-qualification layouts, the immutable v1
attempt above, and the three frozen U1 child episode histories. Reading exact-layout hashes from a
history is not loading or querying its policy.

For each lesson independently, the evaluator visits every candidate in ascending numerical seed
order and accepts a candidate if and only if all of these predeclared conditions hold:

1. The current generator creates a valid instance of the declared lesson.
2. The scripted oracle solves the complete lesson within its declared horizon and produces all
   required lesson evidence.
3. The candidate's protocol-specific exact-layout hash is not already present among the accepted
   candidates for that lesson.
4. The exact-layout hash is absent from that lesson's frozen validation suite.
5. The exact-layout hash is absent from the same lesson's opened-but-unscored v1 confirmation
   attempt.
6. For Local Unlock U1 only, the exact-layout hash is also absent from the frozen U1
   engineering-qualification suite.
7. For Local Unlock U1 only, the exact-layout hash is absent from the union of all exact U1 layouts
   in the three frozen child episode histories.
8. The lesson-specific visibility, mechanics, and oracle-length conditions below hold.

The first 200 accepted candidates, and only those candidates, form that lesson's confirmation
exam. Panel A is accepted positions 1–100 and Panel B is accepted positions 101–200. Panels are
therefore defined by acceptance order, not by a contiguous subrange after rejected candidates are
removed. No shuffle is permitted.

The selector must scan no seed after the 200th acceptance. If a lesson does not produce 200
accepted cases by the end of its frozen 10,000-seed stream, qualification fails and no policy is
loaded. A rejected candidate and its exact reason remain in the raw report. An accepted candidate
cannot later be removed because a policy performs poorly on it.

The U1 history-union exclusion makes every accepted successor U1 exact layout novel to all three
lineages, rather than merely novel to the lineage currently being scored. Navigate and U0 exact
training overlaps remain reported diagnostics and are not filtered: U0 is intentionally a finite
retention task, so post hoc training-novel filtering would change the question being measured.
Geometry overlaps with any history or confirmation and cross-lesson exact overlaps also remain
diagnostics. The selector may not use geometry novelty as an unstated acceptance filter.

## Mechanical qualification and diversity

All accepted cases must preserve the pixel observation and reward contracts. Recurrent state
resets at the beginning of every scored case. Action selection is deterministic, curiosity is
disabled, and success remains the complete lesson objective rather than a milestone.

Every Visible Unlock U0 case must begin with the matching key and locked door visible and have a
complete oracle solution of 5–10 actions. Every Local Unlock U1 case must begin with the key
visible and door hidden, preserve the non-collinearity, minimum-distance, post-key turn, and
locked-divider contracts, and have matching pure-planner and live-oracle solutions of 9–18 actions.

The exact-layout acceptance rule guarantees 200 unique accepted exact layouts per lesson and 100
per panel. The original v1 diversity floors remain frozen and must still be explicitly reported:

| Lesson | Overall exact-layout floor | Exact-layout floor per panel | Additional geometry floor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Navigate | 160/200 (80%) | 80/100 (80%) | Reported, no separate gate |
| Visible Unlock U0 | 160/200 (80%) | 80/100 (80%) | Reported, no separate gate |
| Local Unlock U1 | 198/200 (99%) | 99/100 (99%) | 190/200 overall and 95/100 per panel |

Any solvability, mechanics, observation, reward, horizon, visibility, action-length, accepted-set
uniqueness, U1 geometry-floor, or reference-exclusion failure invalidates v2 before policy scoring.

- the number of candidates consumed before the 200th acceptance for every lesson;
- same-protocol exact-layout overlap against the corresponding U1 child history;
- protocol-independent geometry matrices against the corresponding U0 parent and U1 child
  histories, previously opened U0 and U1 confirmation exams, and among the three new exams; and

Any accepted exact collision with a declared reference exclusion is a qualification failure.
Ordinary finite-generator repeats in candidate streams are rejected deterministically and
reported. Geometry-only overlap, Navigate training overlap, and U0 training overlap are reported
diagnostics and do not change the verdict.

## No-update guarantee

V2 loads one checkpoint at a time into evaluation-only environments only after all three exams have
been selected and qualified. It does not create a training vector environment or call `learn`,
backpropagation, optimizer-step, reward shaping, checkpoint selection, curriculum advancement, or
an online model.

The evaluator runs under inference mode and records canonical policy-parameter and optimizer-state
digests before and after all 600 cases per checkpoint. It requires exact digest equality, unchanged
training counters, unchanged checkpoint bytes, and `policy_updates: false`. A mismatch invalidates
the confirmation.

The launcher creates an exclusive, durable attempt ledger and evaluator log on the T7 before the
first v2 candidate is opened. It finalizes the ledger and hashes any produced report even when the
capability verdict is negative or qualification fails. An interruption therefore remains visible
rather than silently becoming a clean retry.

An engineering interruption may be rerun only with the same evaluator source, checkpoints,
digests, candidate order, acceptance rule, panels, and deterministic settings after the defect is
documented. It cannot justify retuning, checkpoint reselection, threshold changes, accepted-case
removal, or replacement streams.

## Frozen interpretation

- **Confirmed:** integrity and qualification pass and all three policies pass every lesson and
  panel gate.
- **Mixed or negative capability evidence:** integrity and qualification pass, but at least one
  policy misses a lesson or panel gate.
- **Qualification failure:** a candidate stream cannot supply its frozen exam, or an accepted exam
  violates solvability, mechanics, diversity, or a declared evidence-partition requirement.
- **Integrity failure:** checkpoint, lineage, allocation, source, or no-update verification fails.

Only the first outcome supports beginning the next Local-to-Separated Unlock design as a
replicated, disjoint confirmation result. None of the outcomes establishes a complete
dungeon-playing agent.
