# U2r launch attempt 1: zero-action operational failure

> **Result:** the externally preregistered U2r trainer stopped during its initial vector-environment
> reset on July 23, 2026. It performed **zero policy actions**, **zero remediation actions**, and
> **zero optimizer updates**. This is an operational launch failure, not a learning result, a
> stability result, or permission to alter the original U2 verdict.

## Result in one sentence

The original U2r exclusion rule tried to make every training lesson novel to the complete historical
layout inventory; that rule left only 26 of the finite Visible Unlock U0 generator's 2,800 exact
layouts available, and the preregistered worker encountered 128 excluded U0 candidates in a row
before the policy could act.

## Root cause

The original static inventory contained 84,218 exact layout identities across qualification,
development suites, three completed U2 histories, inspectable U2 confirmation selection evidence,
and earlier U1 confirmation evidence. Applying that entire union as a hard guard to every lesson
was defensible for the large or effectively open Navigate, U1, and U2 distributions. It was not
feasible for finite U0.

Visible Unlock U0 has exactly 2,800 possible exact layouts. The original rule excluded 2,774 of
them, leaving 26, or 0.93% of the domain. The inherited scheduler assigned:

1. worker 0 to Local Unlock U1, which accepted the logged layout immediately; then
2. worker 1 to Visible Unlock U0.

Worker stream `20260750` deterministically proposed 128 U0 candidates before the environment's
fixed rejection ceiling. Those candidates represented 126 unique exact layouts, and all 128
proposals belonged to the old exclusion union. The environment therefore raised:

`RuntimeError: could not sample a training layout outside reserved evidence sets`

The guard did exactly what the frozen code required. The protocol had required something the finite
U0 distribution could not reliably supply.

## Claim boundary

Attempt 1 supports one useful engineering conclusion: a collision-safe evaluation protocol does not
automatically imply that full-history novelty is a workable training distribution. It says nothing
about whether 360,448 additional actions improve the policy.

The original U2 confirmation remains `capability_failed`, U3 remains closed, and the fresh U2r
confirmation streams remain unopened. Only a future, fully qualified r1 terminal artifact could
change the prospective activation decision.
