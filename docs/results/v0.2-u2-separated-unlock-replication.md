# Protocol v0.2 U2 Separated Unlock replication result

> Status: completed development-replication result. All three preregistered U2 children reached
> the complete mastery rule on July 23, 2026. This document closes the protected training cohort;
> it does not replace the separately preregistered, collision-aware U2 confirmation required
> before a generalization claim or U3 activation.

## Result in one sentence

Three independently confirmed Local Unlock policies each learned the longer, visibility-unconstrained
Separated Unlock quest while retaining Navigate, Visible Unlock, and Local Unlock, so the frozen
U2 development-replication criterion passed **three lineages out of three**.

The average deterministic U2 score rose from **37.08% before U2 training**, to **47.08% after the
first 32,768 actions**, to **92.08% at mastery**. The three children used 1,933,312 new actions,
61.46% of the 3,145,728-action cohort ceiling.

![Three inherited policies rise from partial U2 transfer to independent mastery](../assets/v0.2-u2-separated-unlock-replication.svg)

This is evidence for repeatable **cumulative development learning** on the frozen U2 validation
distribution. It is not U2 learning from random initialization, a disjoint U2 confirmation,
unrestricted Full Unlock, Retrieve, general puzzle solving, or complete game intelligence.

## The question that was frozen before training

U0 had taught a short key → door → exit ritual with both objects visible. U1 kept the key visible
but hid the door and required local search. U2 changed the geometry rather than the mechanics:

- a 9 × 9 map with a 7 × 7 interior;
- a complete divider whose sole crossing is a locked door;
- exactly two additional valid wall cells;
- a matching key on the approach side and goal on the far side;
- neither the key nor the door guaranteed visible at the start;
- a shortest valid solution of 17–26 primitive actions; and
- a 160-step horizon.

The policy still received only a `56 × 56 × 3` egocentric partial RGB image and its private
256-unit recurrent state. It received no lesson ID, coordinates, map, object label, route,
objective text, visibility flag, oracle distance, demonstration, walkthrough, action mask,
milestone reward, or online model decision. Key pickup and door opening remained diagnostics;
only completing the full ordered quest earned task success.

Each child inherited one complete, independently learned and independently confirmed U1 recurrent
PPO archive, including optimizer state. It then trained through its own interaction experience
with its own algorithm seed and four worker streams:

## The mastery rule

Before update one, each child took a four-lesson diagnostic baseline. Afterward, the exact same
four frozen 80-case exams ran every 32,768 newly trained child actions. Each exam was divided into
two separately gated 40-case panels.

| Lesson | Overall floor | Panel A floor | Panel B floor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Navigate | 68/80 | 34/40 | 34/40 |
| Visible Unlock U0 | 68/80 | 32/40 | 32/40 |
| Local Unlock U1 | 68/80 | 32/40 | 32/40 |
| Separated Unlock U2 | 68/80 | 32/40 | 32/40 |

A child mastered U2 only after **two adjacent post-update normal-practice boundaries** at which all
four lessons passed overall and in both panels, the completed practice window met its frozen
transition allocation, recovery was inactive, and the evaluated archive and sidecar passed their
digest and provenance checks. The two passes had to be separated by another complete 32,768-action
training window. An isolated favorable exam was intentionally insufficient.

## The learning curve was not smooth

The clean headline hides the scientifically useful part of the run. U2 progress repeatedly rose,
fell, and recovered:

| Child | Baseline → first exam | At 524,288 actions | Earlier isolated gate crossing | Final adjacent mastery pair |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `20260737` | 32 → 40 | 65 (33, 32) | 71 (33, 38) at 589,824, then 66 (32, 34) | 69 (34, 35) at 655,360 → 75 (37, 38) at 688,128 |
| `20260741` | 33 → 44 | 73 (36, 37), first pass | none | 73 (36, 37) at 524,288 → 74 (35, 39) at 557,056 |
| `20260745` | 24 → 29 | 64 (34, 30) | 70 (35, 35) at 557,056, then 63 and 67 | 76 (38, 38) at 655,360 → 72 (36, 36) at 688,128 |

For child `20260737`, 71/80 at 589,824 actions was a valid single crossing, but the next exam fell
to 66/80. For child `20260745`, 70/80 at 557,056 was followed by 63/80 and 67/80. Neither temporary
high score became mastery. The controller waited for a new adjacent pair, which is why the retained
`first-pass-separated-unlock` artifacts for those lineages belong to 655,360 actions.

This matters to the interpretation. The result is not “one lucky evaluation per seed.” Each final
claim survived another complete training window and another separately panel-gated exam.

## What the result supports

The result supports this narrow statement:

> Under the frozen U2 protocol and development exams, the unchanged pixel-only recurrent learner
> repeatedly added Separated Unlock to three independent, confirmed U1 lineages while preserving
> every earlier frozen lesson.

Five observations make that statement meaningful:

1. **Transfer was present but incomplete.** The U1 parents began at 24–33 successes, not zero, yet
   all were far below the 68/80 U2 gate.
2. **Learning appeared immediately and continued.** All three improved after one 32,768-action
   window, then eventually reached 72–75/80.
3. **The answer replicated across policy lineages.** Each child had a different learned parent,
   U2 algorithm seed, and worker stream.
4. **Retention was enforced rather than assumed.** Every U2 gate required simultaneous Navigate,
   U0, and U1 passes; automatic recovery reacted twice when those skills weakened.
5. **A lucky peak could not stop training.** Two children crossed the U2 gate once, fell back, and
   had to earn a later adjacent two-exam pair.

This is stronger than “one agent got through a hard room once.” It is still deliberately narrower
than “the agent understands keys and doors everywhere.”

## Limits

- Every child was warm-started from a separately learned and confirmed U1 parent. This experiment
  does not show U2 learning from random parameters or from the beginning of the curriculum in one
  uninterrupted fresh-start run.
- The three policies are independent lineages, but they faced the same fixed 80-case development
  exams. Those cases were repeatedly consulted for recovery and stopping, so they are validation
  evidence rather than an untouched test.
- Scores from the three policies must not be pooled into a 240-case agent. Likewise, thousands of
  training episodes do not increase the replication count beyond three.
- U2 geometry is constrained: one divider, one matching key, one locked door, one goal, and exactly
  two extra walls on a 9 × 9 map. There are no decoy keys, multiple doors, enemies, inventory
  decisions, levers, text, combat, gear, party roles, or multiple floors.
- The terminal visibility-stratum results are telemetry on small, unequal fixed groups. They were
  not separately powered confirmation panels.
- Recovery preserved frozen prerequisite exam performance. It does not prove lifelong immunity to
  catastrophic forgetting under arbitrary future lessons.
- Recurrent memory is inferred from performance under partial observation; this experiment does
  not identify the internal representation or prove human-like planning.
- The sealed qualification proved the generator and oracle contract, not policy capability. Oracle
  behavior was never training data.
- The complete-project final partition beginning at 20 million remained untouched.
- U2 development success alone does not activate U3. The frozen protocol requires a separately
  preregistered, policy-blind, collision-aware U2 confirmation first.

## The narrative

The cleanest story is not “an AI solved a dungeon overnight.” It is:

> Three apprentices already knew a short ritual. We moved the objects apart, hid them behind
> partial vision, and added walls. Each apprentice initially solved only about one third of the new
> rooms. Practice helped, but progress was messy: old skills weakened, automatic rehearsal took
> over, and two apparent breakthroughs disappeared on the next exam. Only after each apprentice
> passed the hard lesson twice in a row—without losing any earlier lesson—did the experiment stop.

That story has four visual beats:

1. **The inheritance:** baseline U2 is 30–41.25%, showing partial reuse rather than a blank slate.
2. **The struggle:** non-monotonic curves and two automatic prerequisite recoveries.
3. **The discipline:** isolated 70/80 and 71/80 scores do not count when the next exam fails.
4. **The payoff:** 3/3 policies finish at 90–93.75% U2 while terminal prerequisite scores remain
   92.5–100%.

The honest final line is equally important: this closes development replication, not generalization.
The next scene is the collision-aware confirmation exam. Its accepted layouts must be absent from
every persisted validation, qualification, prior-confirmation, and completed logged U2 training
history. Closeout also exposed an instrumentation limit: four active worker layouts per lineage
contributed final transitions but were not persisted as episode records. The confirmation plan
therefore does not claim novelty against those 12 layouts, and U3 must record episode starts and
active worker state directly.
