# Protocol v0.2 Local Unlock replication result

> Status: frozen pre-confirmation result record. This document reports the completed U1 development
> child and its two preregistered replications. Later confirmation results belong in a separate
> record; any correction here must be an explicit dated erratum rather than a silent rewrite.

## Result in one sentence

Three independently trained and confirmed Visible Unlock policies each learned the harder Local
Unlock quest while retaining Navigate and Visible Unlock, so the preregistered U1 replication
criterion passed three lineages out of three.

This is evidence for repeatable **cumulative** learning on the frozen U1 development distribution.
It is not a fresh-start U1 result, a disjoint U1 confirmation, unrestricted Unlock, Retrieve, or an
untouched final-suite claim.

## Frozen question and decision rule

The U1 development child inherited the exact confirmed seed-`20260725` U0 policy and its PPO
optimizer, then learned from new experience under protocol `dungeon-apprentice-v0.2-u1`. It produced
a positive result before the two replication children trained. The
[replication plan](../v0.2-u1-replication-plan.md) then froze the other two confirmed U0 parents,
child algorithm seeds `20260729` and `20260733`, non-overlapping worker streams, the existing
524,288-child-action ceiling, and the unchanged U1 training and evaluation contract.

Each child began from its own confirmed U0 parent. It did not inherit the successful lead's U1
weights, trajectories, recurrent episode state, curriculum state, or worker stream.

At every 32,768 newly trained child actions, one frozen deterministic exam measured:

- Navigate on two fixed 40-case panels;
- Visible Unlock U0 on two fixed 40-case panels;
- Local Unlock U1 on two fixed 40-case panels.

Navigate required at least 68/80 overall and 34/40 in each panel. U0 and U1 each required at least
68/80 overall and 32/40 in each panel. U1 mastery required two consecutive post-update boundaries
where:

1. all three lessons passed their overall and panel gates;
2. no prerequisite recovery was active;
3. the completed normal-practice window was within five percentage points of its 50/15/35
   transition targets.

The initial three-lesson baseline was diagnostic and could not count toward mastery. A child counted
as positive only if it reached the complete two-exam gate before the frozen ceiling with valid
qualification, lineage, allocation, evaluation, and checkpoint artifacts. Local Unlock counted as
replicated only if **both** preregistered children were positive. Both were positive; the development
lead was positive as well.

The policy continued to observe only its partial RGB image and private recurrent state. It received
no lesson ID, coordinates, map, object labels, route, objective text, demonstration, oracle action,
walkthrough, or online model decision. Key acquisition and door opening remained diagnostics:
positive task reward still required the complete key-to-door-to-exit sequence.

## Limitations and next test

This result has four important boundaries:

1. **Warm-start transfer:** every U1 child inherited a separately learned and confirmed U0 policy
   and optimizer. The result shows cumulative acquisition across three lineages, not U1 learning
   from random parameters.
2. **One development suite:** all three policies used the same fixed 80-case U1 validation exam.
   This is appropriate for comparing lineages without moving the gate, but repeated use turns it
   into a development suite rather than an untouched generalization test.
3. **Constrained Local Unlock:** the key begins visible, the door begins hidden, geometry forces a
   turn and separation, and oracle solutions span 9–18 actions. This is harder than U0 but still much
   easier than unrestricted Unlock.
4. **Policy-level replication:** evaluation cases from three checkpoints are not independent
   training replications and are never pooled as though they came from one 240-episode policy.

The selected mastery checkpoints must remain frozen. A separate
[U1 confirmation plan](../v0.2-u1-confirmation-plan.md) defines any future disjoint seed blocks,
qualification floors, thresholds, checkpoint selection, and all-three decision rule before those
cases are inspected. Confirmation must perform no policy updates and cannot replace a checkpoint
after seeing its score.

## Precise claim

Protocol v0.2 provides replicated development evidence that a confirmed recurrent visual policy can
extend a short Visible Unlock behavior into a longer, partially observed Local Unlock
key-to-door-to-exit sequence through its own experience while retaining Navigate and Visible Unlock.
The conclusion is supported by three positive parent-to-child lineages, two consecutive frozen exams
per lineage, valid transition-balanced normal and recovery practice, exact parentage, passed
qualification, and digest-linked mastery checkpoints.

The result justifies a preregistered disjoint U1 confirmation. It does **not** establish U1
generalization beyond the development suite, learning from random initialization, U2 Separated
Unlock, U3 Full Unlock, Retrieve, or complete-dungeon competence.
