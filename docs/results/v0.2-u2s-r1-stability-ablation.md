# U2-S r1 Stability Ablation: Terminal Result

> **Result:** all four matched arms completed their exact full budgets, but none passed all five
> capability-and-stability requirements on each of the three fixed terminal exams. The immutable
> cohort verdict is **`ablation_failed`**. No learner configuration was selected, no ablation
> checkpoint may be reused, no successor cohort is authorized, and U3 remains closed.

## Result in one sentence

The unchanged learner kept strong U2 capability but retained rare catastrophic interaction loops;
the no-effect feedback reduced those tails without eliminating them; conservative PPO weakened U2
learning; and combining both interventions removed the tails but held U2 below its capability gate.

This is a valid negative learning result, not a trainer crash or an integrity failure.

## The frozen question

The [U2-S protocol](../protocol-v0.2-u2s-stability-ablation.md) restarted four fresh children from
the same confirmed U1 parent and crossed two mechanisms in a matched 2 × 2 design:

| Arm | PPO rule | Repeated visible no-effect feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `control` | Existing U2 PPO | Off |
| `conservative` | Gentler, decaying PPO updates | Off |
| `no-effect` | Existing U2 PPO | On |
| `combined` | Gentler, decaying PPO updates | On |

All arms used algorithm seed `20260753`, worker streams `20260753`–`20260756`, the same static
training guards, and the same pre-action RNG identity. Each received exactly 1,048,576 new U2
actions. The arms therefore isolate mechanisms under one matched trajectory; they are not four
independent population replications.

Only the exams at 983,040, 1,015,808, and 1,048,576 child actions could decide eligibility. Every
one of those three exams had to:

1. pass all existing prerequisite overall and panel gates;
2. score U2 at least 72/80 with both panels at least 34/40;
3. keep U0, U1, and U2 mean ineffective interactions at or below 3.0;
4. contain no case with 10 or more ineffective interactions; and
5. contain no identical pickup, drop, or toggle run of length 10 or more.

One good terminal exam was insufficient. All three had to pass independently.

### Why each arm failed

- **Control retained capability but not reliability.** It passed every U2 overall and panel gate,
  yet every terminal exam contained at least one forbidden interaction tail. Across the window,
  six cases reached 10 or more ineffective interactions and the worst loop lasted 156 actions.
- **Conservative PPO underlearned U2.** Its three U2 scores were fixed at 64/80, below 72, and its
  weaker panel failed as well. The gentler updates did not reliably suppress tails: the final two
  exams contained loops of 152 and 155 actions.
- **No-effect feedback came closest, but was not stable enough.** Its first terminal exam passed
  every condition at 78/80 with a worst case of two ineffective interactions. The next two exams
  preserved high capability but each produced one forbidden outlier, including a 126-action loop
  in the exact terminal exam. The preregistered rule correctly rejects one clean window followed by
  two brittle ones.
- **Combined feedback solved the measured tail at the cost of capability.** All three terminal
  exams contained zero 10+ cases, their worst case was seven, and the longest repeated identical
  interaction run was two. But U2 remained at 70, 69, and 71, with the weaker panel below 34 every
  time.

The experiment therefore exposed a capability–stability tradeoff inside this learner family. No
cell made both properties coexist at the required fixed boundaries.

The attractive individual windows remain diagnostic evidence only. In particular, the no-effect
arm's clean 983,040-action exam cannot be selected after seeing its later failures, and the
combined arm's terminal stability cannot compensate for missing the capability gate.

The result supports this narrow claim:

> Under the tested recurrent-PPO architecture, neither gentler updates nor bounded pixels-only
> no-effect feedback produced a learner that combined high U2 capability with reliable
> interaction stability across all three fixed terminal exams.

It does not say that U2 is unlearnable. Control and no-effect both retained 76–79/80 U2 capability.
It says that tuning this PPO-and-reward family did not make that capability reliably stable.

## What the negative result changes

The next iteration should not be another rescue continuation, a lower threshold, a larger penalty,
or a retrospective best-checkpoint choice. U2-S supplied enough evidence to retire that search
direction:

- unchanged PPO can learn the quest but occasionally enters catastrophic action loops;
- gentler PPO sacrifices too much capability;
- a reward signal can reduce loops without making the internal behavior reliably self-correcting;
  and
- suppressing loops strongly enough can prevent the agent from learning the full plan.

The next hypothesis belongs at the architecture boundary: give a fresh learner a way to represent
the relationship between its own previous action and the visible consequence, while preserving the
experience-only contract. That is a new prospective protocol, not a continuation of U2-S, and it
must restart from an authorized confirmed boundary rather than any artifact in this ablation.
