# Protocol v0.2 Visible Unlock replication result

> Status: frozen result record. This document reports the completed development sentinel and its
> two preregistered replications. Later confirmation results belong in a separate record; any
> correction here must be an explicit dated erratum rather than a silent rewrite.

## Result in one sentence

Three independently initialized recurrent pixel policies mastered the complete Visible Unlock
quest while retaining Navigate, so the preregistered U0 replication criterion passed three runs out
of three.

This is evidence for repeatable learning on the frozen U0 development distribution. It is not yet
evidence for U1–U3, unrestricted Unlock, Retrieve, or an untouched final suite.

## Frozen question and decision rule

The development sentinel at algorithm seed `20260725` produced a positive result. The
[replication plan](../v0.2-u0-replication-plan.md), written before the next policies trained, froze
two fresh random initializations at seeds `20260726` and `20260727`. Each received at most 524,288
trained actions under the same environment, policy, optimizer, reward, transition scheduler, exams,
and promotion rules. Neither replication resumed the development policy or received its
trajectories.

A replication counted as positive only if it reached the two-exam Visible Unlock mastery gate while
retaining Navigate and satisfying transition-allocation tolerance. The U0 result counted as
replicated only if **both** fresh runs were positive. Both were positive, and the development
sentinel was positive as well.

The policy observed partial RGB pixels and its own recurrent state. Key pickup and door opening were
diagnostics, not rewards: positive task reward still required the complete key-to-door-to-exit
sequence. No demonstration, oracle action, map coordinate, object label, walkthrough, or online
model call entered training.

## Learning trajectory

U0 performance was nonlinear. The policies often remained at or near zero before a rapid increase,
and two policies temporarily regressed between exams before recovering.

The two-consecutive-exam requirement mattered. None of the three policies had a confirmed result at
458,752 actions even though each had crossed the one-exam threshold. The 524,288-action ceiling left
enough room for the confirmation exam at 491,520.

The transition-deficit scheduler corrected the episode-length imbalance found in v0.1. The final
completed post-promotion windows were effectively 50/50, and every completed practice window was
within the frozen five-percentage-point tolerance.

Final Navigate ranged from 91.25% to 93.75%, above the 85% retention requirement in every policy.
No policy needed the automatic 75/25 recovery schedule.

## Validation-diversity limitation

The frozen 80-seed U0 validation exam contains **79 unique layouts**, because two seed cases produce
the same valid geometry. This was disclosed before the replications and deliberately left unchanged
for an apples-to-apples test. Results are therefore reported as 80 deterministic seed cases, not as
80 unique layouts.

The repetition does not explain the result—the same policies succeeded on nearly every seeded
case—but it limits the claim. The replications establish repeatability across optimizer
initializations on one frozen development suite; they do not replace a disjoint-layout confirmation.
A separately preregistered [post-training confirmation](../v0.2-u0-confirmation-plan.md) addresses
that limitation with fresh 200-case blocks and an explicit uniqueness report and qualification
floor.

The development run predates a behavior-neutral reporting correction. Its finalizer repeated the
already graded 491,520-action exam and then displayed an empty allocation window. This report uses
the first scheduled mastery exam and the `mastered-visible-unlock` sidecar, whose allocation is
intact. Reloading that checkpoint reproduced 74/80 Navigate and 79/80 U0 exactly. The two
replications ran after the correction and contain neither presentation defect.

All three runs passed 2,000/2,000 oracle qualification cases: 1,000 Navigate and 1,000 U0 cases. U0

solutions proved solvability but were never supplied to the policies.

The three runs consumed 1,474,560 trained actions and approximately 2 hours 48 minutes of wall time.
Their raw artifacts remain on the T7; this repository record contains the frozen aggregate result,
configuration provenance, and digests rather than model archives.

## Claim

Protocol v0.2 provides replicated evidence that a randomly initialized recurrent visual policy can
learn a short, complete, ordered key-door-exit behavior while retaining an earlier navigation skill
when practice is allocated by transitions. The result supports advancing to a disjoint U0
confirmation and then a harder local-Unlock slice. It does not establish unrestricted Unlock or the
complete dungeon curriculum.
