## Verdict

**Qualification failed before policy scoring.** All three 200-case blocks passed their numerical,
mechanical, solvability, and diversity checks, and the numerical seed ranges were disjoint from all
declared prior ranges. The cross-reference audit nevertheless found nine unique confirmation cases
whose exact generated layouts had already appeared in a declared evidence partition:

- eight Visible Unlock U0 cases matched the fixed U0 validation suite; and
- one Local Unlock U1 case matched the U1 engineering-qualification suite.

The evaluator therefore stopped as required. It did not load or score any of the three selected
policy checkpoints. The report contains `checkpoint_scoring_performed: false`, an empty
`checkpoints` array, and no policy success counts. Consequently, `qualification_failed` is evidence
about the confirmation instrument, **not evidence that any policy could or could not solve
Navigate, Visible Unlock, or Local Unlock**.

The unmeasured capability gates remain exactly as preregistered: each checkpoint would have needed
at least 170/200 Navigate successes and 85/100 in each Navigate panel; at least 170/200 U0 successes
and 80/100 in each U0 panel; and at least 170/200 U1 successes and 80/100 in each U1 panel. No
checkpoint reached that scoring phase, so none of those nine per-policy lesson results exists.

## What the attempt taught us

The first attempt made the distinction between **seed-disjoint** and **layout-disjoint** concrete.
Its numerical partitions were impeccably separate, yet nine cases were exact repeats of layouts
from evidence already used during development. Seed partitioning prevents reuse of the same random
number, but it cannot prove novelty of the generated object unless the generator is injective over
the tested ranges. This generator is not.

That does not erase the clean 3/3 U1 replication result, and it does not turn this attempt into a
negative generalization result. It means the proposed post-training test did not satisfy its own
stronger evidence-partition contract, so the model question was never asked.

Any successor must be explicitly non-retroactive: preserve this attempt and its checksum, publish a
new protocol before opening new cases, use fresh unopened numerical ranges, and define how exact
reference-layout exclusions are handled before policy scoring. It may not delete the nine cases,
substitute favorable seeds inside this report, loosen the original rule, or score the policies and
call that attempt 1. A prospectively preregistered successor is justified because no checkpoint was
scored and therefore no negative capability result is being rerun; it is a new, better-qualified
measurement instrument, not a rewrite of the first one.
