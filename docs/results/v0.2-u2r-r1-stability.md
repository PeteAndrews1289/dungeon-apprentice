## Why a healthy mean concealed a dangerous tail

The penultimate U2 exam contained 15 ineffective interactions across 80 cases. Its worst individual
case had four, and no case had ten.

The terminal U2 exam contained 333:

| Terminal U2 tail | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Cases with any ineffective interaction | 21/80 |
| Cases with at least 10 | 3/80 |
| Maximum in one case | 153 |
| Total ineffective interactions | 333 |
| Mean | 4.1625 |

Two deterministic layouts produced **299 of the 333 interactions, or 89.8% of the total**:

| Validation seed | Outcome | Loop | Ineffective | Steps | Quest state reached |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `11_200_025` | Timeout | 145 repeated toggles | 146 | 160 | Key acquired and door opened |
| `11_200_027` | Timeout | 153 repeated pickups | 153 | 160 | Neither key nor door milestone |

The first policy had effectively solved the ordered interaction sequence and then toggled until
timeout. The second repeatedly attempted pickup from an unchanged situation. These are not broad
failures to understand Separated Unlock: the policy still completed 76/80 cases and reached 79
keys and 78 doors. They are rare but catastrophic deterministic tails.

The same terminal exam also contained one Local Unlock case with 42 ineffective interactions and a
41-action repeated run. Its lesson mean remained only 0.5875 because the other 79 cases were clean.
This is why a successor mechanism study must record case-level tails and streaks, not only means.

## Decision and claim boundary

The exact terminal artifact is **not eligible** for
`dungeon-apprentice-v0.2-u2r-stability-confirmation`. The reserved
`15_240_000`–`15_279_999` candidate streams were never opened, scored, or reassigned. The final
allocation beginning at 20 million remains untouched.

The attractive penultimate artifact cannot replace the terminal artifact. Its 76/80 U2 result and
0.1875 ineffective mean are useful evidence that the learner can enter a stable regime, but using
it after seeing the terminal regression would violate the terminal-only rule. Continuing r1,
lowering 3.0, choosing the best-looking intermediate checkpoint, or launching another U2r
continuation would all rewrite the declared experiment after the answer.

The result supports this narrow statement:

> Under unchanged PPO and reward, additional ordinary experience preserved strong U2 capability
> but did not reliably preserve interaction stability at the fixed terminal boundary.

It does not support U3, Retrieve, a full game, or a claim that the original U2 cohort confirmed
3/3. The historical record remains:

1. all three U2 lineages mastered the development gate;
2. two of three passed the larger confirmation;
3. the failed lineage later learned a cleaner U2 behavior under bounded remediation;
4. its exact terminal artifact nevertheless failed one prospective stability check; and
5. the one fresh remediation confirmation stayed sealed.

## Prospective next question

U2r answered that simply adding more unchanged PPO experience is not a reliable solution. The next
step is therefore a mechanism experiment, not another rescue continuation. The
[U2-S stability ablation](../protocol-v0.2-u2s-stability-ablation.md) prospectively crosses:

- unchanged versus conservative PPO updates; and
- no added feedback versus a pixels-only penalty for repeated visibly ineffective interactions.

It starts four fresh matched children from a frozen confirmed U1 parent—not from any U2 or U2r
artifact—and selects only a learner configuration. Its checkpoints cannot activate U3.
