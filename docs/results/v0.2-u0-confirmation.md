## Interpretation

The development finding survived a larger disjoint-layout test. The weakest U0 checkpoint scored
93.5%, comfortably above the frozen 85% requirement, while preserving 94.5% Navigate. The other two
U0 policies scored 99.5% and 99.0%. The result is therefore not explained by one favorable random
initialization or the single repeated geometry in the original validation set.

The confirmed capability remains intentionally small: the key and door begin visible, solutions are
short, and the geometry is constrained. The next justified question is U1 Local Unlock, where the
same growing policy must retain Navigate and U0 while solving a longer complete interaction chain.
