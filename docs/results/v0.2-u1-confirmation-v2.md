## Verdict

and Local Unlock overall and in both fixed 100-case panels. Every checkpoint passed all nine
lesson-level gates, no weights or optimizer state changed, and no online model was used.

policy-blind, exact-reference-disjoint test. It does not establish learning from random parameters

## Interpretation

The central result survived two independent challenges:

1. **Replication:** three separate U0 parents produced three U1 children that mastered the same
   cumulative development gate.
2. **Reference-disjoint confirmation:** those already selected checkpoints then passed larger U1
   exams whose exact layouts were absent from validation, engineering qualification, attempt 1, and
   all three U1 training histories.

The scores are stronger than the original 72/80 mastery values without being selected for that
outcome: U1 confirmation reached 94%, 96%, and 91%, while Navigate remained 94%–96% and U0 was
perfect for all three policies. The evidence supports the narrow claim that recurrent visual
policies learned and retained a complete, longer, partially observed Local Unlock sequence rather
than memorizing only the fixed development exam.

Attempt 1 remains scientifically useful. Its qualification failure exposed the difference between
seed-disjoint and layout-disjoint evidence. V2 did not delete bad cases or retry after a poor policy
score; attempt 1 never loaded a policy. The successor acceptance rule was committed before its new
candidate layouts were opened, and rejected cases were determined without policy behavior.

## Limits

- Every U1 child was warm-started from a separately learned and confirmed U0 parent. This is
  cumulative transfer, not U1 learning from random initialization.
- Local Unlock remains constrained: the key begins visible, the door begins hidden, geometry forces
  separation and a turn, and oracle solutions span 9–18 actions.
- All three policies faced the same accepted exams. They are independent trained policies, but
  their 200 evaluation cases are not three independent test samples and must not be pooled.
- U1 exact layouts were novel to all three child histories. U0 deliberately was not filtered
  against training history: 179, 189, and 175 of its 200 accepted layouts appeared in the
  corresponding child histories. U0 therefore confirms retention on validation- and
  attempt-disjoint layouts, not exact training-layout novelty.
- Protocol-independent geometry repetition remained diagnostic. U0 shared 39 geometries with
  attempt 1 and 42 with the earlier U0 confirmation; those are not exact-layout collisions under
  the declared hashing contract.
- The selector conditions the test distribution on solvability, mechanics, exact uniqueness, and
  declared exclusions. The result applies to that preregistered conditioned Local Unlock
  distribution, not arbitrary dungeons.
- No claim is made about U2/U3, Retrieve, combat, inventory strategy, multi-agent party behavior, or
  the untouched final suite beginning at 20 million.
