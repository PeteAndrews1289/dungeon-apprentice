# V0.2 U0 post-training confirmation plan

> Status: preregistered before confirmation. The checkpoint identities, seed blocks, panel splits,
> qualification floor, thresholds, and decision rule below are frozen. No confirmation layout or
> policy score was generated or inspected while writing this plan.

## Question

All three independently initialized policies mastered Visible Unlock on the frozen development
validation suite. Do the already selected mastery checkpoints retain Navigate and solve Visible
Unlock on larger, disjoint deterministic seed blocks that played no role in training, promotion,
checkpoint selection, or replication reporting?

This is a post-training confirmation, not another training run. Policy weights, recurrent-policy
settings, environment rules, and action selection remain unchanged throughout evaluation.

## Frozen confirmation partitions

Each checkpoint receives the same two deterministic 200-case exams. Recurrent state resets at the
start of every case, and the policy uses deterministic action selection.

| Lesson | Exact seed block | Panel A | Panel B | Cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Navigate | `15_000_000`–`15_000_199` | `15_000_000`–`15_000_099` | `15_000_100`–`15_000_199` | 200 |
| Visible Unlock U0 | `15_010_000`–`15_010_199` | `15_010_000`–`15_010_099` | `15_010_100`–`15_010_199` | 200 |

The Navigate range was already reserved for fresh confirmation. This plan newly reserves the U0
range. Neither block may be shortened, extended, shuffled, substituted, or supplemented after any
policy is scored.

The development U0 validation suite had 79 unique geometries among 80 seed cases. Confirmation must
therefore publish the unique layout count and layout hashes for each 200-case lesson block. Each
block must contain at least **160 unique layouts (80%)** and all 200 cases must pass the scripted
solvability qualification before policy evaluation is considered valid. Qualification actions are
never policy inputs or training data.

If a frozen block misses its uniqueness or solvability requirement, the outcome is a qualification
failure. The block is not silently replaced after scores or layouts have been observed; any successor
confirmation requires a new public preregistration.

## Frozen pass thresholds

Every checkpoint must independently satisfy all four requirements:

| Lesson | Overall requirement | Panel A requirement | Panel B requirement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Navigate | at least 170/200 (85%) | at least 85/100 (85%) | at least 85/100 (85%) |
| Visible Unlock U0 | at least 170/200 (85%) | at least 80/100 (80%) | at least 80/100 (80%) |

The confirmation is **positive** only if all three frozen checkpoints pass both lessons overall and
every panel floor, all archive digests match, all allocation records pass verification, and both
seed blocks pass qualification. One checkpoint failure makes the strict three-policy confirmation
negative, while the complete per-policy and per-panel scores remain reportable as mixed evidence.

An engineering interruption may be rerun only with the same checkpoint, digest, seed order,
deterministic evaluation settings, and evaluation code after the defect is documented and fixed. It
cannot justify retuning the policy, selecting a different checkpoint, changing a threshold, or
choosing replacement seeds.

## Required report

The post-run record must include, for every checkpoint and lesson:

- exact successes out of 200, overall rate, and both 100-case panel counts;
- key-pickup and door-open rates for U0 as diagnostics, without treating them as success;
- unique layout count, qualification outcome, and seed-block identity;
- checkpoint path, frozen SHA-256, measured SHA-256, and digest verdict;
- final-window transition counts and shares plus the all-window tolerance verdict;
- deterministic-evaluation setting, source commit, runtime, and any interruption;
- the strict all-three decision without pooled-agent statistics replacing individual results.

No weights are updated and no result from this confirmation may be used to reselect or retune the
three policies. A positive result supports beginning U1 Local Unlock; it still does not establish
U1–U3, unrestricted Unlock, Retrieve, or final-suite generalization.
