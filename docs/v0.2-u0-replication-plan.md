# V0.2 Visible Unlock replication plan

> Status: preregistered launch plan. Run only after the duplicate-final-exam reporting correction is
> committed and the working tree is clean. The correction may change reporting but must not change
> the environment, policy, reward, optimizer, curriculum, seeds, or promotion gates.

## Question

The development sentinel at seed `20260725` mastered Visible Unlock. Its final frozen exams scored
92.5% on Navigate and 98.75% on Visible Unlock after 491,520 trained actions. Is that result
reproducible in two independently initialized policies under the same protocol and budget?

An independent checkpoint reload reproduced those scores exactly. Every recorded checkpoint digest
matched its file, qualification passed 2,000/2,000 cases, and the post-promotion allocation was
50.0295% Navigate / 49.9705% Visible Unlock. The audit also found two presentation defects: the
already-graded mastery boundary was evaluated a second time during finalization, and the completed
allocation window was then displayed as empty. The behavior-neutral correction skips a duplicate
same-boundary final exam and preserves the completed mastery window. Regression tests freeze both
properties before these replications launch.

These replications test repeatability of the implemented U0 slice. They do not establish mastery of
the proposed U1–U3 staircase or the complete dungeon curriculum.

The runs execute sequentially on the audited 8 GB M1. Each uses one four-worker CPU trainer. This
preserves the sentinel's compute conditions and avoids the slowdown and memory pressure previously
observed when trainers competed. The second dashboard becomes live only after the first run ends.

Both policies begin from random parameters. Neither resumes the successful sentinel or receives its
trajectories. The launcher refuses an existing run path, a dirty working tree, occupied dashboard
ports, an unmounted T7, or a source change between seeds.

The frozen 80-seed Visible Unlock examination contains 79 distinct layout hashes because two seeds
generate the same valid layout. This satisfies the preregistered development qualification and is
kept unchanged for an apples-to-apples replication; counts are reported as seeded exam cases, not as
80 unique geometries. The future full U0–U3 confirmation retains its stronger diversity requirement.

Seeds `20260726` and `20260727` are new to the project. The paired `20260722`–`20260724` seeds remain
reserved for the preregistered complete U0–U3 study, so this development replication does not consume
or selectively preview that future comparison.

## Frozen settings and decision rule

Every behavior-affecting option is identical to the development sentinel: recurrent PPO from pixels,
the same network and optimizer settings, 32,768-action post-update exams, 80 frozen validation
layouts per active lesson, and complete key-to-door-to-exit success as the only positive task reward.
Key pickup and door opening remain diagnostics rather than rewards.

Navigate promotion requires two consecutive exams at or above 90% overall and 85% in each 40-layout
panel. Visible Unlock mastery requires two consecutive exams at or above 85% overall and 80% in each
panel while Navigate remains at or above 85%. The scheduler must keep completed practice windows
within five percentage points of their frozen transition targets. The automatic retention-recovery
rule remains active.

A replication is positive only when the run reaches the existing Visible Unlock mastery gate before
its 524,288-action ceiling with valid transition allocation. A run reaching at least 25% final
Visible Unlock without mastery is partial. Below 25% is an interaction failure. Broken allocation,
qualification, checkpointing, or evaluation is reported separately as an operational or engineering
failure and is not converted into a learning result.

The U0 learning result is considered replicated if **both** fresh seeds are positive. One positive
and one non-positive run is mixed evidence. Neither run may be manually stopped because its curve
looks favorable or unfavorable; early stopping occurs only through the frozen mastery gate.

## Launch and resource expectation

From the repository root:

```bash
scripts/run_v02_u0_replications.sh
```

The original sentinel took 54 minutes and occupied 442 MiB. Two sequential replications should take
roughly two hours and approximately 0.9–1.0 GiB, with variation from their mastery time and retained
checkpoints. At preregistration the T7 had 218 GiB available, in addition to the launcher's existing
25 GiB free-space guard.

After both runs, report every frozen exam trajectory, first mastery boundary, final Navigate and U0
counts, both panel counts, transition allocation, wall time, artifact digest, and source commit for
each seed. Summarize across policies; do not pool all evaluation episodes as if they were one agent.
