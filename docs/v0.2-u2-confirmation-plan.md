## Question

Do the three already selected first-mastery U2 checkpoints retain Navigate, Visible
Unlock, Local Unlock, and Separated Unlock on deterministic, collision-aware cases
from numerically reserved streams, excluded against validation, qualification, prior
confirmation, and every completed logged training history promised below? Because 12
active final worker layouts were not persisted, exact collision with those partially
trained layouts cannot be ruled out and is not part of the novelty claim.

This is a post-training measurement. It never changes a policy, optimizer,
curriculum, recurrent state, reward, generator, or checkpoint selection. A positive
result confirms replicated cumulative Separated Unlock on the declared distribution.
It does not establish U3 Full Unlock, Retrieve, arbitrary planning, the untouched
final allocation, or party behavior.

## Why another evaluation is required

The three U2 children all satisfied the frozen two-boundary development mastery rule.
Those 80-case validation suites were nevertheless measured every 32,768 new trained
actions and controlled recovery, mastery counting, and stopping. They are learning
evidence, not a disjoint post-training confirmation.

The U1 confirmation attempt also showed that different numerical seeds can generate
the same exact layout. Confirmation therefore uses large ordered candidate streams,
policy-blind exact-layout exclusions, deterministic acceptance order, and panel
qualification before a policy can load.

## Deterministic selection and panels

For each lesson independently, candidates are visited in ascending seed order. A
candidate is accepted if and only if:

1. its seed belongs to that exact lesson's reserved stream and the persisted attempt
   capability authorizes it;
2. the declared 9 × 9 generator produces a valid instance;
3. the pixel observation remains `56 × 56 × 3` `uint8` and the action space remains
   the same seven primitive actions;
4. a separately executed live oracle completes the full lesson within its frozen
   horizon;
5. every lesson visibility, divider, wall, ordering, and oracle-length contract holds;
6. for Local and Separated Unlock, the pure planner and live oracle action counts
   match;
7. its exact layout is absent from every gating exclusion for that lesson; and
8. its exact layout is not already accepted for that lesson.

The first 200 accepted cases form the exam. Panel A is accepted positions 1–100 and
Panel B is positions 101–200. No shuffle is permitted. Selection stops immediately
after acceptance 200. Failure to accept 200 cases within the frozen 10,000 candidates
ends the attempt as `qualification_failed`; no policy loads.

Every visited seed gets a durable pre-inspection opening record, terminal inspection
status, and rejection reason when rejected. Exact-layout hash, geometry hash,
oracle-action count, and non-policy visibility diagnostics are retained whenever
inspection completes far enough to produce them—always for accepted cases and
collision-rejected cases. Earlier mechanical/generation failures retain nulls for
fields that could not be established. Oracle action sequences are not retained as
demonstrations.

## No-update guarantee

No policy is deserialized until all four exams qualify. The evaluator then loads one
checkpoint at a time on CPU, uses deterministic actions under inference mode, resets
recurrent state at every case, and disables curiosity.

For each checkpoint it records and requires exact before/after equality of:

- checkpoint archive bytes;
- checkpoint sidecar, integrity record, and frozen source-evidence files;
- canonical policy-parameter digest;
- optimizer-state digest;
- model timestep and optimizer-update counters; and
- checkpoint identity and selection position.

During scoring, after all four policy-blind exams qualify, the evaluator also requires
the three canonical loaded policy-tensor digests to be distinct. This separates the
pre-access serialized-member proof from the later deserialized learned-parameter proof.

The evaluator has no training environment and never calls `learn`, gradient
backpropagation, optimizer step, reward shaping, curriculum advancement, checkpoint
selection, an oracle action as policy input, or an online model. Any mutation makes
the attempt an operational integrity failure rather than a capability result.

## Interpretation

- `confirmed`: all qualification, integrity, no-update, and all-three capability
  gates passed. This authorizes design work on a separately frozen U3 protocol.
- `capability_failed`: the instrument was valid but at least one policy missed a
  frozen capability gate. This is mixed or negative confirmation evidence.
- `qualification_failed`: panels could not be constructed under the prospective
  mechanical, collision, or diversity rules; no policy score exists.
- `integrity_failed` or `interrupted`: the attempt cannot answer the capability
  question.

No outcome from this confirmation opens the final allocation or establishes U3,
Retrieve, new mechanics, or complete-dungeon competence.
