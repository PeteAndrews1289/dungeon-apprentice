> Status: preregistered before either replication child received a policy update. The successful
> seed-`20260725` lead remains development evidence until both lineages below finish.

## Question

The lead child added Local Unlock to a confirmed U0 policy: its frozen U1 score rose from 0/80 at
baseline to two consecutive passing exams of 73/80 and 72/80 while Navigate and Visible Unlock
remained above their gates. Can the same unchanged training protocol repeat that result from the
other two independently trained and independently confirmed U0 policies?

This is a replication of cumulative learning, not a fresh random initialization. Each child inherits
one exact U0 policy and its optimizer. It does not inherit the successful U1 policy, trajectories,
recurrent episode state, demonstrations, oracle actions, or curriculum progress.

## Frozen protocol

Environment, pixels, actions, recurrent policy, optimizer, generator, reward, curiosity, validation
layouts, lesson gates, and recovery controller remain identical to the lead protocol. In particular:

- every child receives at most 524,288 new trained actions;
- a 1,000-case Local Unlock qualification must pass before training;
- the initial Navigate/U0/U1 exam is diagnostic and cannot count toward mastery;
- normal practice is 50% Navigate, 15% U0, and 35% U1 by actual transitions;
- every 32,768-action boundary examines all three lessons on the same frozen 80-case suites;
- Navigate requires 68/80 overall and 34/40 in each panel;
- U0 and U1 each require 68/80 overall and 32/40 in each panel;
- U1 mastery requires two consecutive post-update boundaries where all three lessons pass and the
  completed normal allocation window is within five percentage points of every target;
- prerequisite recovery is permitted and reported, but recovery exams cannot count toward U1;
- early stopping is permitted only at the complete cumulative mastery gate.

Before update one, the launcher and runner require a clean committed source tree, the frozen raw U0
confirmation digest, the exact parent archive and sidecar, 491,520 inherited trained actions, 960
optimizer updates, full optimizer state, the declared parent/child pairing, a new run directory, and
at least 25 GiB free on the T7. The deterministic Navigate/U0 baseline must reproduce the selected
parent's frozen result exactly: 75/80 and 80/80 for parent `20260726`, then 73/80 and 80/80 for parent
`20260727`.

## Decision rule

A child is positive only if it reaches the existing two-exam U1 mastery gate before its ceiling with
valid lineage, allocation, and artifacts. A rise without mastery is a transfer signal, a final U1
score below 25% is an interaction/transfer failure, and broken provenance, qualification,
checkpointing, evaluation, or allocation is an engineering failure rather than a learning result.

Local Unlock learnability is considered replicated only if **both** new children are positive. That
would produce three positive policy lineages out of three including the lead. One positive child is
mixed evidence; zero leaves the lead as an unreplicated development success. Evaluation episodes are
never pooled as though they came from one policy.

No run may be manually stopped because its curve appears favorable or unfavorable. Any
behavior-affecting correction invalidates both replications and requires a new declared protocol.
