# V0.2 U1 post-training confirmation plan

> Status: preregistered before confirmation. The checkpoint identities, seed blocks, panel splits,
> qualification floors, gates, and decision rule below are frozen. No confirmation layout, oracle
> result, policy action, or policy score was generated or inspected while authoring this plan.

## Question

Three independently trained U0 parents each produced a warm-start child that mastered Local Unlock
while retaining Navigate and Visible Unlock. Do those three already selected U1 checkpoints retain
all three capabilities on larger deterministic blocks that played no role in training, promotion,
mastery selection, or replication reporting?

This is post-training confirmation. It never changes a policy, optimizer, curriculum, recurrent
state, reward, or generator. A positive result confirms replicated cumulative learning on the Local
Unlock distribution; it does not establish U2, unrestricted Unlock, Retrieve, or final-suite
generalization.

## Frozen confirmation partitions

Every checkpoint receives the same three deterministic 200-case exams. Each ordered block is split
into its first and second 100 cases; cases are not shuffled between panels.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Local Unlock U1 | `15_020_000`–`15_020_199` | `15_020_000`–`15_020_099` | `15_020_100`–`15_020_199` | 200 |
| Navigate | `15_030_000`–`15_030_199` | `15_030_000`–`15_030_099` | `15_030_100`–`15_030_199` | 200 |
| Visible Unlock U0 | `15_040_000`–`15_040_199` | `15_040_000`–`15_040_099` | `15_040_100`–`15_040_199` | 200 |

The U1 block was reserved by the development protocol and remained unopened through training and
replication. The Navigate and U0 blocks are newly reserved here. They do not reuse the already
opened U0-era confirmation blocks at 15.00 million and 15.01 million, and they do not consume the
project's 20-million final partition.

No block may be shortened, extended, reordered, supplemented, or replaced after qualification or
scoring begins. Recurrent state resets at the beginning of every case. Action selection is
deterministic, curiosity is disabled, and success remains the complete lesson objective rather than
a milestone.

## Qualification before policy scoring

The evaluator qualifies all 600 cases before loading any selected policy. All 200 cases in every
lesson must be solved by the scripted oracle, remain inside their declared horizon, preserve the
pixel observation and reward contracts, and produce their declared lesson evidence. Oracle actions
are qualification evidence only and never become policy inputs or training data.

The following diversity floors are frozen:

| Lesson | Overall exact-visual floor | Exact-visual floor per panel | Additional geometry floor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Navigate | 160/200 (80%) | 80/100 (80%) | Reported, no separate gate |
| Visible Unlock U0 | 160/200 (80%) | 80/100 (80%) | Reported, no separate gate |
| Local Unlock U1 | 198/200 (99%) | 99/100 (99%) | 190/200 overall and 95/100 per panel |

Every U0 case must begin with the matching key and locked door visible and have a complete oracle
solution of 5–10 actions. Every U1 case must begin with the key visible and door hidden, preserve the
non-collinearity, minimum-distance, post-key turn, and locked-divider contracts, and have matching
pure-planner and live-oracle solutions of 9–18 actions.

Qualification publishes every seed's protocol-specific exact-visual hash, protocol-independent
geometry hash, oracle length, visibility result, duplicates, and panel membership. A solvability, mechanics, observation,
reward, horizon, visibility, action-length, or uniqueness-floor failure invalidates this
confirmation before a policy is scored. The frozen seed block is not quietly replaced; a successor
requires a new public preregistration.

## Seed and layout collision accounting

Seed partitions and generated layouts are different concepts. The evaluator must publish a literal
interval table and pairwise proof that the three numerical seed blocks do not overlap training, the
U1 engineering qualification, Navigate/U0/U1 validation, either prior U0 confirmation block, one
another, or the 20-million final boundary.

The repository's historical exact-layout hashes include protocol and generator salts, so an exact
hash is comparable only inside the same hashing contract. The evaluator therefore publishes the
strongest honest collision evidence available:

- protocol-specific exact-layout overlap against every episode in the corresponding U1 child;
- exact-layout overlap against the frozen same-protocol Navigate, U0, and U1 validation suites and
  the Local Unlock engineering-qualification suite, with a declared reference overlap invalidating
  qualification;
- protocol-independent geometry matrices against every episode in the corresponding U0 parent and
  U1 child, reconstructed where the older history did not record geometry directly;
- protocol-independent geometry matrices against the previously opened U0 confirmation blocks and
  among the three new lesson blocks; and
- explicit exact-layout and geometry duplicates overall and within both 100-case panels.

The report does not relabel a geometry-only match as an exact visual match across protocols.

A procedurally repeated layout under a different, properly partitioned training seed is reported
honestly per lineage and lesson. It is not turned into a retroactive training failure or silently
removed from the fixed confirmation block. Geometry-only overlap is likewise a diagnostic rather
than automatic invalidation.

An actual seed-range collision is invalid. An exact validation or qualification collision is also
invalid when it contradicts an already declared exact-hash exclusion or evidence partition. The
report must distinguish those contract violations from ordinary finite-generator layout
collisions; it may not claim that seed separation guarantees every geometry is novel.

## Frozen pass thresholds

Each checkpoint must independently satisfy every overall and panel requirement:

| Lesson | Overall requirement | Panel A requirement | Panel B requirement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Navigate | at least 170/200 (85%) | at least 85/100 (85%) | at least 85/100 (85%) |
| Visible Unlock U0 | at least 170/200 (85%) | at least 80/100 (80%) | at least 80/100 (80%) |
| Local Unlock U1 | at least 170/200 (85%) | at least 80/100 (80%) | at least 80/100 (80%) |

The strict confirmation verdict is **positive only if all three frozen checkpoints pass all three
lessons overall and all six panel floors**. One policy failure makes the strict all-three verdict
negative, while every individual count remains reportable as mixed evidence. Results are never
pooled into a synthetic 600-case policy.

## Checkpoint, lineage, and allocation integrity

Before a score can count, the evaluator must verify for each selected checkpoint:

- the measured archive SHA-256 equals the frozen digest above and the schema-3 mastery sidecar;
- protocol is `dungeon-apprentice-v0.2-u1`, curriculum is mastered, and the recorded child boundary
  is the first two-exam mastery boundary;
- child seed, U0 parent seed, parent archive digest, parent source, and U1 source match the frozen
  lineage;
- the U0 confirmation report is the passed, no-update artifact with SHA-256
  `f43610252943fce9c0169ac0231724fc2829686d1899f5f88bd0c250a913b398`;
- inherited progress is 491,520 trained actions and 960 optimizer updates before the child segment;
- the final completed normal-practice window targets 50% Navigate, 15% U0, and 35% U1 and remains
  within five percentage points of every target;
- every earlier completed practice or recovery window satisfies its declared target tolerance;
- the selected sidecar, manifest, curriculum events, deterministic evaluations, episode history,
  and engineering qualification match the SHA-256 values frozen before confirmation;
- the three selected policy parameter digests are distinct; and
- archive, policy, observation, action, LSTM, and optimizer metadata are internally consistent.

Any failed digest, parentage, first-mastery, allocation, or artifact check is an operational
integrity failure, not a capability result.

## No-update guarantee

Confirmation loads one checkpoint at a time into evaluation-only environments. It does not create a
training vector environment or call `learn`, backpropagation, optimizer-step, reward shaping,
checkpoint selection, or curriculum advancement. It makes no online model call.

The evaluator runs under inference mode and records canonical policy-parameter and optimizer-state
digests before and after all 600 cases, requiring equality along with unchanged training counters
and checkpoint bytes. The raw result must state `policy_updates: false`. A mismatch invalidates the
confirmation.

## Required report

The immutable post-run record must include:

- exact successes out of 200 and both 100-case panel counts for every checkpoint and lesson;
- U0 and U1 key-pickup, door-open, and quest-completion counts, plus `door_given_key` and
  `success_given_door` where denominators are nonzero;
- all qualification, visibility, oracle-length, exact-visual uniqueness, and U1 geometry-uniqueness
  results by block and panel;
- numerical seed-partition verification and the exact-layout/geometry collision diagnostics scoped
  above;
- selected path, frozen archive digest, measured archive digest, policy-parameter digest, parent
  lineage, source commit, and integrity verdict for every checkpoint;
- final-window transition counts and shares, maximum deviation, and the all-window allocation
  verdict, plus whole-child transition totals and shares as non-gating telemetry;
- deterministic-evaluation settings, evaluator source commit, start/end timestamps, runtime,
  interruption history, and before/after policy digests;
- the per-policy result and strict all-three verdict, without pooled performance replacing either;
  and
- the raw report path and an externally measured SHA-256 sidecar.

The launcher creates an exclusive, durable attempt ledger and evaluator log on the T7 before the
first confirmation case is opened. It finalizes that ledger and hashes any produced report even
when the frozen capability verdict is negative or qualification fails. An interrupted attempt
therefore remains visible rather than silently becoming a clean retry.

An engineering interruption may be rerun only with the same evaluator source, checkpoints, digests,
seed order, panels, and deterministic settings after the defect is documented. It cannot justify
retuning, checkpoint reselection, threshold changes, case removal, or replacement seeds.

## Frozen interpretation

- **Confirmed:** all integrity and qualification checks pass and all three policies pass every
  lesson and panel gate.
- **Mixed or negative capability evidence:** integrity and qualification pass, but at least one
  policy misses a lesson or panel gate.
- **Qualification failure:** a frozen seed block violates solvability, mechanics, diversity, or a
  declared evidence-partition requirement.
- **Integrity failure:** checkpoint, lineage, allocation, source, or no-update verification fails.

Only the first outcome supports beginning the next Local-to-Separated Unlock design as a replicated,
disjoint confirmation result. None of these outcomes establishes a complete dungeon-playing agent.
